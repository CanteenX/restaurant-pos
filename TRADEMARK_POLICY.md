- Say truthfully that you use, host or support Nventra POS.

### Modified versions and forks
- Do not present a modified or independently built version as an official

- Give a fork its own name and its own logo. "Based on Nventra POS" or "a fork
  of Nventra POS" is fine.
- Keep the copyright and licence notices (see `NOTICE`), including the upstream
  Nodedr OrderRestro ones, and mark your changes and their dates, as the AGPL
  requires.
- Replace the in-app branding and the source-code link in Settings with your own.

Use of the Nventra marks in a product or company name, domain name, app-store
listing, or in a way that suggests an official relationship needs written
permission. Ask by opening an issue on
[CanteenX/restaurant-pos](https://github.com/CanteenX/restaurant-pos/issues).
